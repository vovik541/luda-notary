About Liudmyla Petruk

A California Notary Public in Citrus Heights who works in English, Ukrainian and Russian.

How she works

Liudmyla notarizes documents by appointment in Citrus Heights and on mobile visits across Sacramento and Placer counties. Identity first, then the signature or oath, the journal entry and the certificate, with each step explained. She does not give legal advice.
