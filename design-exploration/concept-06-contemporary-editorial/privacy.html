Privacy & terms

How information from this site and appointments is handled. Draft for review.

Draft legal text for review. Replaces the PDF on the current site. Effective date, service providers and Do Not Track statement to be completed before launch.

What is collected

Details you send by form, phone, text or email: name, phone, email, city and appointment details. At an appointment, the identification details California law requires in the notary journal.

How it is used

To answer your request, arrange and carry out the appointment, and keep records the law requires. Your information is not sold.

Sharing

Only with providers that run this website and its form (named at launch), or where the law requires.

Calls and texts

Only if you tick the optional box, Liudmyla may call or text you about your request. Ask her to stop at any time.

Notary journal

Journal entries are kept and released only as California law allows.

Your choices

Write to mila.petruk.ca@gmail.com to see or correct your information.

Terms of service

An appointment is confirmed once time, place and fees are agreed. Payment is due at the appointment by Zelle, cash or check. Notarization confirms identity and signature, not the content or legal effect of a document. I am not an attorney and, therefore, cannot give legal advice about immigration or any other legal matters.
