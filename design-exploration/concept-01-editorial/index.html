What Liudmyla notarizes

Six everyday notarial services, each explained before you sign anything.

Mobile notary visits

Liudmyla comes to your home, office or another agreed place in the service area.

You cannot easily travel, several people need to sign together, or you simply prefer to sign at home.

Acknowledgments

Confirms your identity and that you signed the document willingly.

Deeds, agreements, trusts, powers of attorney and many forms that say "acknowledged before me".

Jurats

You swear or affirm that a document is true, then sign it in front of the notary.

Affidavits, sworn declarations and other statements made under oath.

Power of attorney notarization

Notarizing a power of attorney you have already prepared.

Financial, durable and other powers of attorney that need a notarized signature.

General document notarization

Everyday personal and business documents that need a notarized signature.

Consent letters, business forms, vehicle and school documents, and similar paperwork.

Real estate documents

Notarizing deeds and other documents that affect real property.

Grant deeds, quitclaim deeds, deeds of trust and related forms.

All services in detail

From first call to signed page

  1. Tell her what you need

    Call, text or send the form with the document type, number of signers and where you would like to meet.

  2. Confirm the details

    Liudmyla confirms the time, the place and the full fee before the appointment. Nothing is booked until both sides agree.

  3. Meet and sign

    Bring every signer, acceptable ID for each one and the complete, unsigned document.

    Pay by Zelle, cash or check at the appointment. The notary journal entry and certificate are completed on the spot.

Before she arrives

A current government photo ID for every signer
For example a California driver license or ID card, a U.S. passport, or another ID accepted under California law. It must be current or issued within the last five years.
The complete document, unsigned
Do not sign in advance unless the document says otherwise. All pages should be present.
Everyone who needs to sign
Each signer must appear in person and be able to communicate directly with the notary.
Credible witnesses, if a signer has no ID
California allows identification by credible witnesses in some cases. Ask Liudmyla before the appointment.

Zelle, cash or check, for the fee confirmed before the appointment.

Questions people ask first

How much does a mobile notary appointment cost?

California law sets the maximum notarial fee: up to $15 per signature for an acknowledgment and up to $15 per person for a jurat oath. Travel is a separate fee, quoted individually and confirmed with you before the appointment.

What ID do I need?

A current government-issued photo ID, such as a California driver license or ID card or a U.S. passport, or another ID accepted under California Civil Code §1185. Each signer needs their own ID.

Can you tell me which document I need or how to fill it in?

No. A notary is not an attorney and cannot give legal advice or choose documents for you. For legal questions, please speak with an attorney.

Do you offer same-day or weekend appointments?

Same-day appointments may be available after confirmation. Weekends are possible only by prior arrangement, for an additional fee agreed in advance.

Can I come to you instead?

Yes, by appointment in Citrus Heights. The exact address is shared when your appointment is confirmed.

How do I pay?

By Zelle, cash or check at the appointment, after the fee has been confirmed with you. No payment is requested before the appointment is confirmed.

Which languages do you speak?

Liudmyla speaks English, Ukrainian and Russian, so she can communicate directly with signers in any of the three.
