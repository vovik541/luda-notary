Questions, by service

Notary

How much does it cost?

Up to $15 per signature for an acknowledgment or per person for a jurat, the California maximum. A mobile travel fee is quoted and agreed before booking.

Do you come to me?

Yes. Mobile notarization covers 16 cities and communities in Sacramento and Placer counties. You can also meet Liudmyla in Citrus Heights by appointment.

What ID do I need?

A current government photo ID such as a California driver license or ID card or a U.S. passport, or another ID accepted under Civil Code §1185.

Same day or weekends?

Same-day visits may be possible by prior agreement. Weekends only by arrangement, for an additional agreed fee.

Can you tell me which document I need?

No. A notary cannot give legal advice or choose documents. Ask the receiving agency or an attorney.

Document assistance Draft

Are you a lawyer?

No. A Legal Document Assistant is not an attorney and cannot give legal advice, legal strategy or representation.

Can you tell me which form to use?

No. You choose the documents; the LDA prepares them at your specific direction.

Is this service mobile?

Document assistance is by appointment. Mobile visits are not offered for this service unless confirmed later.

How is the price set?

Each job is quoted and written into a contract before work begins. Notary fees, if a notarization is also needed, are separate.
