Questions, answered

LiudmylaNotary Public · general information, not legal advice

Hi, I’m Liudmyla. Pick a question and I’ll answer here.

What should I bring?
A current government-issued photo ID for every signer (driver’s license, state ID or passport) and the complete document. Please leave the signature lines blank unless I tell you otherwise: a jurat must be signed in front of me.
How much does it cost?
The notarial fee is up to $15 per signature for an acknowledgment, or per person sworn for a jurat, the California maximum. I quote travel before booking, and you pay at the appointment by Zelle, cash or check.
Do you come to me?
Yes. I travel to homes, offices and other agreed places across Sacramento and Placer counties. You can also meet me in Citrus Heights by appointment.
Can you give legal advice?
No. As a California notary I can’t give legal advice or decide which document you need. For that, please speak with an attorney.
Can you help prepare my documents?
Document assistance is a separate service, prepared at your direction and quoted in a written contract. Internal draft Details are being confirmed before publication.
Which languages do you speak?
English, Ukrainian or Russian, whichever is easiest for you.
Can I text you first?
Of course. Text me at +1 (916) 301-3829 with your city and the type of document.
