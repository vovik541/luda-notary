About Liudmyla Petruk

A California Notary Public who works by appointment from Citrus Heights and travels across Sacramento and Placer counties.

Liudmyla speaks English, Ukrainian and Russian. In reviews, clients describe clear explanations, punctuality and attention to detail.

She notarizes signatures. She does not give legal advice, and she says so before you sign.
