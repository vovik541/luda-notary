Mobile notary

Liudmyla brings the notary journal and certificates to your home, office or another agreed place.

When a mobile visit helps

  • Several people need to sign together
  • A signer cannot easily travel
  • You would rather sign at home or at work

Good to know

Travel is quoted individually and agreed before booking. Same-day visits may be possible by prior agreement; weekends by arrangement for an additional fee.

How a visit works

  1. Call, text or send a request

    Tell Liudmyla the document type, how many people will sign and where you want to meet.

  2. Agree time, place and fee

    The notarial fee and any travel fee are confirmed before anything is booked.

  3. Show ID and sign

    Each signer appears in person with acceptable ID; the oath or acknowledgment is taken.

  4. Journal and certificate

    The act is recorded in the journal and the certificate completed. Pay by Zelle, cash or check.

Before she arrives

  • Current government photo IDA California driver license or ID card, a U.S. passport, or another ID accepted under California Civil Code §1185. It must be current or issued within the last five years.
  • Every signer, in personEach signer must appear and be able to communicate directly with the notary.
  • The complete, unsigned documentAll pages present. Do not sign beforehand unless the document says otherwise.
  • No ID? Ask firstCalifornia allows identification by credible witnesses in some situations. Call before booking.
