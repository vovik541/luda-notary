Six services, each explained in plain words: what it is, when you need it, and what happens.

Mobile notary visits

Liudmyla comes to your home, office or another agreed place in the service area.

When you need it

You cannot easily travel, several people need to sign together, or you simply prefer to sign at home.

What happens

Appointments are arranged by phone, text or the request form. Travel is quoted individually and confirmed with you before the appointment.

Fee: up to $15 per signature or per person sworn, plus travel for mobile visits.

Acknowledgments

Confirms your identity and that you signed the document willingly.

When you need it

Deeds, agreements, trusts, powers of attorney and many forms that say "acknowledged before me".

What happens

You sign (or confirm your signature) in front of the notary, show acceptable ID, and the notary completes a California acknowledgment certificate.

Fee: up to $15 per signature or per person sworn, plus travel for mobile visits.

Jurats

You swear or affirm that a document is true, then sign it in front of the notary.

When you need it

Affidavits, sworn declarations and other statements made under oath.

What happens

The notary gives the oath or affirmation, you sign in her presence, and she completes the jurat certificate.

Fee: up to $15 per signature or per person sworn, plus travel for mobile visits.

Power of attorney notarization

Notarizing a power of attorney you have already prepared.

When you need it

Financial, durable and other powers of attorney that need a notarized signature.

What happens

California notaries take a thumbprint in the notary journal for powers of attorney. The notary does not draft the document or advise which kind you need.

Fee: up to $15 per signature or per person sworn, plus travel for mobile visits. Read the full guide

General document notarization

Everyday personal and business documents that need a notarized signature.

When you need it

Consent letters, business forms, vehicle and school documents, and similar paperwork.

What happens

Bring the complete, unsigned document. If you are unsure which notarial act your document needs, ask the agency or person who will receive it.

Fee: up to $15 per signature or per person sworn, plus travel for mobile visits.

Real estate documents

Notarizing deeds and other documents that affect real property.

When you need it

Grant deeds, quitclaim deeds, deeds of trust and related forms.

What happens

California requires the notary to record a thumbprint in her journal for documents affecting real property.

Fee: up to $15 per signature or per person sworn, plus travel for mobile visits.
