Power of attorney notarization

For a power of attorney you already have. Liudmyla notarizes the signature. She does not write the document or give legal advice.

Call +1 (916) 301-3829TextRequest appointment

The notary will

  • Check the ID of the person giving the power
  • Confirm they understand and sign willingly
  • Complete the California acknowledgment
  • Take a thumbprint in her journal (required for powers of attorney)

The notary will not

  • Choose the type of power of attorney
  • Write or change the wording
  • Explain its legal effect

For those questions, speak with an attorney.

Questions

Can it be signed in a hospital?

Hospital and care-facility visits are pending confirmation. The signer must communicate directly with the notary and sign willingly.

What ID do I need?

A current government-issued photo ID, such as a California driver license or ID card or a U.S. passport, or another ID accepted under California Civil Code §1185. Each signer needs their own ID.

Can you tell me which document I need or how to fill it in?

No. A notary is not an attorney and cannot give legal advice or choose documents for you. For legal questions, please speak with an attorney.
