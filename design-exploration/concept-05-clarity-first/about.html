About Liudmyla Petruk

California Notary Public · Citrus Heights, CA

Liudmyla notarizes documents by appointment at her Citrus Heights office and at clients' homes and workplaces across Sacramento and Placer counties.

She speaks English, Ukrainian and Russian. She checks identity carefully, records every act in her journal and explains each step. She does not give legal advice.
