Power of attorney notarization

For a power of attorney that is already prepared. Liudmyla verifies identity and notarizes the signature.

Call +1 (916) 301-3829Request appointment

The notary will

  • Check the ID of the person granting the power
  • Confirm they understand and sign willingly
  • Complete the California acknowledgment certificate
  • Record a journal thumbprint, as California requires for powers of attorney

The notary will not

  • Choose the type of power of attorney
  • Write or change the wording
  • Explain its legal effect

Preparing documents is a different service (Legal Document Assistance, internal draft). Legal advice requires an attorney.

Cost

Up to $15 per signature, plus travel for a mobile visit.

Where

Your location or Citrus Heights by appointment.

Bring

Photo ID and the complete, unsigned document.

Questions

Can it be signed in a hospital?

Hospital and care-facility visits are pending confirmation. The signer must communicate directly with the notary and sign willingly.

What ID do I need?

A current government photo ID such as a California driver license or ID card or a U.S. passport, or another ID accepted under Civil Code §1185.

Can you tell me which document I need?

No. A notary cannot give legal advice or choose documents. Ask the receiving agency or an attorney.

Ready to book a notary?

Call or text. Liudmyla confirms the time, place and fee before anything is booked.
