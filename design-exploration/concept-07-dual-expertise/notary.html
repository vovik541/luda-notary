What a California notary does

A California Notary Public is commissioned by the Secretary of State to verify the identity of people signing important documents, confirm they sign willingly, give oaths, and record each act in a journal. A notary does not draft documents, give legal advice or confirm that a document is legally correct.

How notarization works

  1. Call, text or send a request

    Tell Liudmyla the document type, how many people will sign and where you want to meet.

  2. Agree time, place and fee

    The notarial fee and any travel fee are confirmed before anything is booked.

  3. Show ID and sign

    Each signer appears in person with acceptable ID; the oath or acknowledgment is taken.

  4. Journal and certificate

    The act is recorded in the journal and the certificate completed. Pay by Zelle, cash or check.

Identification to prepare

Bring the original ID. Copies and photos are not accepted.

  • Current government photo IDA California driver license or ID card, a U.S. passport, or another ID accepted under California Civil Code §1185. It must be current or issued within the last five years.
  • Every signer, in personEach signer must appear and be able to communicate directly with the notary.
  • The complete, unsigned documentAll pages present. Do not sign beforehand unless the document says otherwise.
  • No ID? Ask firstCalifornia allows identification by credible witnesses in some situations. Call before booking.

Notary questions

How much does it cost?

Up to $15 per signature for an acknowledgment or per person for a jurat, the California maximum. A mobile travel fee is quoted and agreed before booking.

Do you come to me?

Yes. Mobile notarization covers 16 cities and communities in Sacramento and Placer counties. You can also meet Liudmyla in Citrus Heights by appointment.

What ID do I need?

A current government photo ID such as a California driver license or ID card or a U.S. passport, or another ID accepted under Civil Code §1185.

Same day or weekends?

Same-day visits may be possible by prior agreement. Weekends only by arrangement, for an additional agreed fee.

Can you tell me which document I need?

No. A notary cannot give legal advice or choose documents. Ask the receiving agency or an attorney.

How do I pay?

Zelle, cash or check at the appointment, after the fee was confirmed.

Call or text. The time, place and fee are confirmed before anything is booked.
