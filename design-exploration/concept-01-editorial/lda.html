What this role allows

Preparing documents at the client's specific direction and providing general published information. It does not include choosing forms, legal advice or representation.

How it would work

  • You decide which document you need and what it should say
  • A written contract states the service and the price before work begins
  • Liudmyla prepares the document as you direct
