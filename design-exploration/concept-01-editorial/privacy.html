Privacy policy

Effective date: to be set at launch. This page explains what information is collected through this website and appointments, and how it is used.

What is collectedHow it is usedSharingCalls and textsNotary recordsYour choicesTerms of service

What is collected

Information you send through the request form, by phone, text or email: your name, phone number, email, city and appointment details. At an appointment, the identification details California law requires for the notary journal.

How it is used

To reply to your request, arrange and carry out the appointment, and keep records the law requires. It is not sold.

Sharing

Only with service providers that run this website and its form (to be listed at launch), or when the law requires it.

Calls and texts

If you tick the optional box on the form, Liudmyla may call or text you about your request. You can ask her to stop at any time.

Notary records

California requires notaries to keep a sequential journal. Journal entries are kept as the law requires and released only as the law allows.

Your choices

Ask to see or correct the information held about you by writing to mila.petruk.ca@gmail.com. This site's response to browser Do Not Track signals will be stated here at launch.

Terms of service

Appointments are confirmed once time, place and fees are agreed. Payment is due at the appointment by Zelle, cash or check. Notarization confirms identity and signature, not the content or legal effect of a document. I am not an attorney and, therefore, cannot give legal advice about immigration or any other legal matters.
