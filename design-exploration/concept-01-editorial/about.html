About Liudmyla Petruk

A California Notary Public in Citrus Heights, working by appointment and travelling across Sacramento and Placer counties.

Liudmyla speaks English, Ukrainian and Russian. Clients in reviews describe the same things: she explains each step clearly, arrives on time and makes the paperwork feel manageable.

She notarizes signatures. She does not give legal advice, and she says so before you sign.

How every appointment is handled

Identity first
Each signer shows acceptable ID or is identified by credible witnesses, as California law requires.
Willing, informed signers
The signer must communicate directly with the notary and sign of their own free will.
A journal entry for every act
Including a thumbprint for powers of attorney and real property documents.
Fees agreed first
The full fee is confirmed before the appointment.
