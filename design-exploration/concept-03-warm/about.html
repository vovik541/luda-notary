Hello, I'm Liudmyla.

I'm a California Notary Public based in Citrus Heights. I help people sign important documents properly, at their home or office or at mine.

If Ukrainian or Russian is your first language, it can help to hear each step explained in it. I speak English, Ukrainian and Russian.
