Legal document assistance

Help preparing your own legal documents, at your specific direction. Separate from notary services.

What a Legal Document Assistant does

A Legal Document Assistant (LDA) is registered with a California county to help people who represent themselves prepare legal documents, at the client's specific direction. An LDA is not an attorney: an LDA cannot give legal advice, choose forms for you, explain your legal rights or represent you.

Assistance that may be offered

  • Document preparation at your direction

    Typing and preparing documents based on the information and choices you provide.

  • Form completion assistance

    Completing forms you have selected, using the answers you give.

  • Filing assistance, where permitted

    Help with submitting completed documents to an office, where the law allows. Scope to be confirmed.

  • Other self-help document services

    Additional client-directed services only after confirmation of scope.

All items subject to confirmation of scope and verification of registration.

Client-directed process

  1. Describe what you need

    General document type only. Please do not send document contents or ID numbers in the first message.

  2. Written contract and quote

    The service and price are set out in a written contract before any work begins.

  3. You direct the preparation

    You provide the information and decide what each document says.

  4. Review, sign and file

    You review the prepared documents. Signing, notarization and filing are separate steps.

By appointment

Document assistance is by appointment. It is not offered as a mobile service, and remote or online assistance is not offered unless confirmed later. If a prepared document also needs notarizing, that is a separate notarial act with its own fee.

Questions

Are you a lawyer?

No. A Legal Document Assistant is not an attorney and cannot give legal advice, legal strategy or representation.

Can you tell me which form to use?

No. You choose the documents; the LDA prepares them at your specific direction.

Is this service mobile?

Document assistance is by appointment. Mobile visits are not offered for this service unless confirmed later.

How is the price set?

Each job is quoted and written into a contract before work begins. Notary fees, if a notarization is also needed, are separate.
